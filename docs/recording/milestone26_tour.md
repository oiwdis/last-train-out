# Milestone 2.6 gameplay recording

[Play/download the MP4](milestone26_tour.mp4).

Captured from the running Godot 4.5.1 game using `tools/record_milestone26.tscn`: Forward+, **Low**, **854×480**, offline Movie Maker at **24 FPS**, then encoded as H.264/AAC. The fixed capture rate determines playback speed; it is **not a measured real-time frame rate**. See the separate [performance report](../PERFORMANCE_MILESTONE26.md) for uncapped samples. The [matched screenshot gallery](../screenshots/milestone26/README.md) uses High at 1280×720.

The tour shows the authored survivor, walk/sprint/jump, V perspective changes, cab departure, walking aboard the moving crew car, Warehouse A searches, first- and third-person coal carrying, dropping and recovering the same sack, depositing supplies into the train hold, Warehouse B and the freight yard. Location jumps are explicit tour cuts. Scripted movement and E/V input drive the actual game systems; the recording does not replace gameplay with rendered cutscenes.

The recorder asserts successful cab acceleration, remaining aboard while walking, both completed searches, discovery/pickup/recovery of the same cargo, opening the train hold and storing exactly one coal sack. Its completion log and media metadata are under [validation/milestone26](../validation/milestone26/).

The video also exposes unfinished visual work: simplified station/freight architecture, approximate cargo grips and animation polish. It is evidence of the current asset upgrade, not a claim that the photorealistic quality target is complete.
