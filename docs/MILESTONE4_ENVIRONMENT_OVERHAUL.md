## Integration and physical baseline

This development branch starts at `684df38f0d9ba39a876d4788696855f0b64d5b29`, the open, unmerged head of draft PR #6. The Milestone 4 PR targets `integration/milestone-3-5-photoreal-survival`. Renderer acceptance still depends on the integration PR. [Integration PR](https://github.com/oiwdis/last-train-out/pull/6) · [Renderer acceptance evidence](PERFORMANCE_MILESTONE354.md)

The owner observed white track at Harlow, a gap between brick and green wall sections in Bonded Stores, abrupt ground edges on curves, a flat advancing fog front and sparse composition at Ashford. Blackwater needs more recognizable settlement and objective landmarks.

On the physical 2020 M1, native Metal Medium ran at approximately 60 FPS both in ordinary gameplay and in fog. High ran at approximately 30 FPS normally and 20–25 FPS in fog. These are the owner’s manual observations of the integration build. They establish the Medium target for the revised game.
