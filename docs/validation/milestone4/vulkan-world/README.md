# Cold High Vulkan scene preparation

Two hosted Apple Silicon Macs ran the exact build 111 application at 960 × 540 with a private empty writable app cache for every start. Each host rotated four treatments through eight positions per treatment. Both used the same signed pack, camera fixture and integration shader bytes. The complete scene, the existing preparation views and the first 30 playable frames ran in each case. The temporary hidden-corridor and shader substitutions existed only in this diagnostic. The clustered failures occurred on one of the two hosts. Substituting the old fog shader and hiding the corridor each left reproducible losses. Positioning the initial fog camera at the first covered platform view kept every visible asset and completed all sixteen launches. The result supports testing that placement in the real macOS High Vulkan player. Half the original controls were also on a host which had no losses in any arm. The experiment cannot identify the GPU command that timed out or establish a zero crash rate.

The trace recorded the same 82 cumulative specialization pipelines by the end of the first platform view in every completed pair of phases. With the camera at the origin, the fog proxy added 41 and the subsequent platform draw added another 41. Starting at the platform added all 82 during the proxy and zero after its removal. The median platform phase fell from 2.72 s to 0.28 s on the host that reproduced losses and from 2.15 s to 0.14 s on the other host. The preceding proxy phase grew from 4.27 s to 6.60 s and from 3.22 s to 5.09 s, respectively. The following Warehouse A draw added 25 specializations in both arms. These timings describe preparation while the simulation is paused. In build 118 the macOS Vulkan player starts the fog proxy at the platform if High is the first preparation in the process. Any later High preset switch or Continue uses the historical world origin. Build 117 also applied the platform placement on subsequent High requests and failed the ordinary exported preset switch on two Macs. [Window and lifecycle failures](../build-117/README.md) It still draws the full uncovered platform, every interior, and the final gameplay camera through the renderer's queue. Native Metal continues to start at the platform on every preset and can still use the historical origin for its controlled comparison. Low and Medium Vulkan still begin at the origin. A phase marker records the first view selected by the actual player. On two additional Macs, the original camera lost the device on 2/16 and 9/16 cold starts. Starting at the platform completed 16/16 on each. The original and revised cameras shared the same signed pack and the same subsequent camera schedule.
