# First Medium platform frame in build 115

The first platform redraw after switching from Low to Medium took 665.573 ms on the candidate repeat. The next frame took 11.657 ms and the slowest of the remaining 59 took 12.658 ms. The first pass at the same location took 6.192 ms. The matched integration build recorded 19.284 and 50.422 ms on its two passes. A normal player launch directly into Medium took at most 18.087 ms in its first 30 cold frames and 18.441 ms in the warm run. [Every preset and pass](preset-handoff.csv) · [All frame intervals and raw logs](raw-environment.zip)

The instrumented renderer bracket occupied 664.838 ms of the outlier. The process bracket took 0.413 ms, physics took 0.707 ms and reported render setup took 0.084 ms. During the preceding camera transition, both packs added eight draw pipelines and 15 specializations relative to the instant the preset reported ready. Neither counter advanced during the 60 measured platform frames. The same counter changes occurred on the fast candidate pass. The device exposed no per-frame GPU timestamps. These results locate the delay within rendering after a preset and camera change. They cannot distinguish a queued driver operation from synchronization or scheduling.
